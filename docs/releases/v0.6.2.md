Draft notes for the release after 0.6.1. The version number is not decided yet.

## New

- Share a save: *Saves* in the launcher (*Export Save*, *Import a Save*) and *Saves* on the Game tab of the settings
  menu write your save into one zip in your Downloads folder, with the game's log and settings for a bug report, and
  import a zip someone shared. Attach the bug report zip to a report from a place in the game, so it can be reached
  and checked quickly. An import copies your current save to the `saves` folder of the game folder first; in the game
  it takes effect at the next start.
- Customize Launcher (on the launcher's home screen): six colour schemes, a background picture of your own with
  adjustable dimming, animations (a gliding cursor, sliding screens, drifting sparks) and sound effects: the game's
  own cursor, select and back sounds, read from your game. The sounds are off until you turn them on.
- Three more settings take effect without a restart: HDR between off and the tone mapper (only the game's native
  HDR still waits for one), the camera's stick sensitivity, and the speakers (stereo or 5.1).

## Fixed

- Settings menu, mouse: a setting changes only on the two framed arrows beside its value, and the arrows stay in one
  place. A click anywhere on a row used to step the setting, and the left arrow moved with the value's width, so
  clicking through the values landed on the row and stepped the wrong way. Holding the button on an arrow repeats.
